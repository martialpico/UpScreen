Our Story

Authentic Indian dining, reimagined.

AAMAYA Indian Restaurant, set on the beautiful waterfront at Portside Wharf, offers one of Brisbane's most authentic Indian dining experiences. For over a decade, our passion has been preserving India's rich food heritage while presenting each dish with modern elegance.

Using carefully selected spices and premium ingredients, our chefs craft every meal fresh, from flavourful tandoori specialties and handcrafted naan to beloved classics like Butter Chicken, Lamb Rogan Josh and Chicken Tikka Masala.

Whether you're enjoying a relaxed waterfront dinner, celebrating with family, or ordering premium Indian takeaway, AAMAYA delivers warm hospitality and an unforgettable taste of India in every bite.
