Nossa história

Born in Brazil. Brisbane is home now.

O Brasileiro started with a simple kind of homesickness: the smell of black beans simmering all morning, the crackle of torresmo, a café com leite that tastes like Sunday. We bottled that feeling and set it down in the middle of the city, on Adelaide Street.

Everything is cooked the way our families cook it: rump cap grilled to order, farofa toasted golden, açaí blended thick and cold. No shortcuts, no fuss, just generous, honest Brazilian food from morning coffee to late-night caipirinha.
