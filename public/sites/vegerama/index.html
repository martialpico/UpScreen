          Family recipes, since 2009.

          Vege Rama started as one small café on Queen Street, opened by someone who couldn't find anywhere in Brisbane to eat the food she grew up with. In 2012 we opened on Adelaide Street, fully vegan from day one.

          "I taught myself to cook using my mother and grandmother's recipes and techniques."

          Ruchi, founder

          Every day we still cook that way: fresh produce, no shortcuts, and a menu that keeps moving so the regulars never get bored.

          Feeding the office?

          Plant-based lunch catering for meetings and events in the CBD, plus whole vegan cakes for birthdays and farewells. Tell us the date and the headcount, we'll do the rest.
