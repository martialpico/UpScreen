Why an APD

The highest
nutrition standard

Accredited Practising Dietitians (APDs) hold Australia's highest nutrition qualification — university-educated allied health professionals who can assess and treat nutrition-related conditions, not just offer general advice.

Unlike general nutritionists, only APDs qualify for Medicare and government-funded care plans, so your support can be both expert and accessible.
