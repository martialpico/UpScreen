Strength & return to activity

Rehabilitation
& exercise.

Rehabilitation helps you manage musculoskeletal, neurological and orthopaedic conditions — restoring mobility, building strength, optimising how you move and supporting a safe return to activity and sport.

Book an appointment →View clinic services
Pathways

Three routes,
one strong finish

We match the setting to your diagnosis, stage of recovery and goals — and often blend them as you progress.

🧘

Clinical Reformer

Therapist-supervised reformer work with adjustable resistance and support. Ideal for lower back, hip and knee pain, balance training and post-operative recovery — building control, flexibility, strength and function.

🏋

Gym-Based

Strength conditioning grounded in evidence: resistance and functional training, balance work and return-to-sport conditioning for tendon injuries, sporting injuries, chronic conditions and post-op recovery.

🏠

Home-Based

Structured programs you perform independently — focused on mobility, flexibility, strength, balance or symptom management, often alongside clinical or gym-based work.

Hydrotherapy facility

Hydro Health
Pool, Ascot

Our hydrotherapy sessions run from the Hydro Health Pool in Ascot — a warm-water facility ideal for low-impact, post-surgical and rehabilitation exercise. The buoyancy of the water takes load off healing joints, so you can move freely, rebuild strength and regain confidence sooner.
