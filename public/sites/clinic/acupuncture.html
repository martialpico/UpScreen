Acupuncture.

Fine, sterile needles placed at targeted points to ease pain, calm tension and support your body's natural recovery. Used on its own or woven into your wider treatment plan, acupuncture is a gentle way to help the body settle and reset.

Acupuncture is a complementary therapy. We'll always talk through your history first and let you know when another treatment — or your GP — is the better path.

What to expect

Gentle,
considered, calm

Your practitioner assesses what's going on, explains the plan, then places a few fine needles at chosen points. Most people feel only a light sensation and spend the session resting quietly. Single-use, sterile needles are used every time.

Acupuncture or myotherapy?

Acupuncture works through fine-needle point stimulation to calm pain and tension; myotherapy uses hands-on soft-tissue techniques to release tight muscle directly. Many people combine the two, and we'll help you choose. About myotherapy →
